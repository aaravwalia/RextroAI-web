What it does

Every screen below is the real app, not a mock-up.

Billing

Bills in eight seconds, one bill per table

Staff tap the photo of the dish — a new worker can bill on day one. A table that keeps ordering stays as one running order with its own number, so five rounds of chai still end in a single bill.

Staff purchases

You approve what your staff spends

The worker photographs the bill on their own phone and types the amount. It reaches you instantly. Nothing counts as spent until you approve it, and the photo is deleted the moment you do.

Stock

Stock that counts itself down

Tell it what each dish uses once. After that every cold coffee takes the milk out, every sandwich takes the bread, and you can see what is left without walking to the back.

Profit

What you kept, not what you sold

Sales, minus what the ingredients cost, minus a day's share of rent, salaries and bills. The number at the bottom is the real one, for today, this week or this month.

Also inside

Staff can't delete orders

Every order is logged with time, items and payment mode. Staff can't remove or edit one — only you can.

Attendance

Staff clock in and out on their own phone. You see who worked and for how long.

Regulars and discounts

Customers by phone number, their visits and spend, and a discount that applies itself at the counter.

Purchases and bills

Every purchase in one list by date, with supplier and quantity, downloadable as CSV.

Item sales

How many of each dish sold today, this week or this month, by category.

Two logins

Owner signs in with Google. Staff use a six digit PIN and never see your money screens.

What it does not do

Better you know now than after you have paid.

  • No GST e-invoicing.
  • No thermal or KOT printer — receipts go to the customer on WhatsApp.
  • No Zomato or Swiggy integration.
  • No table map or floor plan — running orders are numbered and can be named.
  • One shop per login. A second outlet is a second login.
  • It needs an internet connection to sync.

Questions people ask

Do I need to buy a billing machine?

No. It opens in the browser on the phone or tablet you already have. Nothing from an app store, nothing to update.

Will my staff see my profit?

No. Staff log in with a PIN and only get the billing screen and the purchase form. The money screens are yours alone — that separation is enforced in the database, not just hidden on screen.

What happens to the bill photos my staff send?

The full photo is deleted as soon as you approve the purchase. A small preview and the typed amount stay on the record.

What if the internet goes for a few minutes?

Short drops are fine — the app keeps a local copy and syncs when the connection returns. A shop with no working data connection at all is not a good fit.

Can you change something for my shop?

Usually yes. Message me with what you need and I will tell you honestly whether it is a small change or a separate build, and what it would cost.
