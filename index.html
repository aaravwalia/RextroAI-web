A furniture shop is the same sum

One sofa can be a day’s takings, and at the end of the day you still want the one number: what did the shop keep? Most of this page already works for a shop that sells pieces instead of plates. Here is exactly how much of it, and exactly what I am still changing.

Works today, unchanged

  • Your staff bill from their own phones with their own PIN, and see nothing else: not your margins, not your accounts.
  • A catalogue of pieces, each with its selling price, what it cost you, and a photo so staff pick it by sight instead of hunting a code.
  • The payment code on the screen already has the amount in it, so nobody keys ₹42,000 as ₹4,200.
  • A boy buys polish, fittings or a load of transport at the market, photographs the bill, and it waits for you to approve before it touches your accounts.
  • The evening and the month: sold, less what the pieces cost you, less rent and salaries. The figure at the bottom is what you kept.
  • Customers’ numbers and their visits, so the family that bought a bed hears from you when they furnish the next room.
  • Your data out as a file whenever you ask, whether you stay or go.

What I am changing now

  • The words. It says menu and Point of Sale today, because the first shop that ran it was a cafe. It will say catalogue and counter.
  • Counting pieces, not kilos. The stock screen is built for milk and flour. A furniture shop needs to know it has three of that sofa left and one still coming.
  • The table card comes off. Nobody orders a wardrobe from a QR code on a table, and you should not pay for a screen you will never open.

It is a few weeks of work, and I would rather do it with a real shop open in front of me than guess at it. If that is your shop, you get it set up in person, you get a say in what it does, and you get a price that reflects going first.

The things people ask

Do I need to buy a machine?

No. It runs in the browser on any Android phone or tablet the shop already has. If your staff can use WhatsApp, they can use this.

Will I have to type my whole menu in?

No, I do it. Send me a photo of your menu and it is in before I arrive. That is the part everyone dreads and it is not your job.

Can my staff see what I earn?

No. Staff log in with their own PIN and get the billing screen. The accounts, the margins and the profit screen are not on their phone at all.

What if the internet goes?

Billing keeps working on the phone and the sales go up when the signal comes back. In a long outage, take the money and bill it when you are back — nothing is lost.

Is my data mine?

Yes. Ask any time and I send you everything — sales, stock, customers — as a file you can open in Excel. That holds whether you stay or leave.

Does it do GST?

Not yet, and I would rather say so than find out together. If you need GST invoices today, this is not the right thing for you this month.

Does it work for a furniture shop?

The billing, the staff PINs, the purchase approvals, the payment code and the profit sum already do. I am changing the words, the way stock counts pieces instead of kilos, and dropping the table card. Ask me and I will tell you honestly where it stands.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing dramatic. The month you have paid for finishes, I export your data and send it, and that is that. No lock-in, no notice period.
